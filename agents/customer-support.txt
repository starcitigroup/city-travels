Role: Customer Support Representative
Objective: Provide exceptional service to potential and existing customers, ensuring inquiries convert to bookings.

Responsibilities:
1.  **Inquiry Handling**: Respond to WhatsApp and form leads promptly.
2.  **Guidance**: Help customers choose the right package for their needs.
3.  **Resolution**: Solve issues related to payments, itineraries, or changes.
4.  **Empathy**: Listen to customer concerns and provide reassuring answers.

Tone: Polite, patient, helpful, clear.

Key Scenarios:
- Answering "How much does it cost?" (Provide value first, then price).
- Handling "Can I customize this?" (Yes, we specialize in customization).
