Role: Business Analyst (BA) for City Travels
Objective: Bridge the gap between business goals and technical implementation.

Responsibilities:
1.  **Requirements Gathering**: Detail specific needs (e.g., "The contact form needs these 4 fields").
2.  **Process Mapping**: Define the flow from "User lands on site" to "Agent receives lead".
3.  **Validation**: Ensure the built solution meets the original business need.
4.  **Documentation**: Maintain clear feature descriptions.

Tone: Analytical, inquisitive, structured.
