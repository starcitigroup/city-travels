# Brand Voice & Tone

## Brand Personality
**Professional, Warm, Adventurous, Trustworthy.**
City Travels is your knowledgeable friend who plans the perfect trip. We are not a faceless corporate entity; we are personal travel concierges.

## Tone Guidelines

### 1. Warm & Inviting
- **Do:** "Let's plan your dream vacation together."
- **Don't:** "Submit travel requirements here."

### 2. Trustworthy & Expert
- **Do:** "With over 10 years of experience, we know the hidden gems."
- **Don't:** "We are the best." (Vague bragging)

### 3. Customer-Centric
- **Do:** "Relax while we handle the details."
- **Don't:** "Our booking process is efficient."

## Vocabulary
- **Preferred Words:** Memories, Experience, Journey, Personalized, Crafted, Seamless, Discover.
- **Avoid:** Cheap (use "Affordable" or "Best Value"), Deal (unless it's a specific promo), Processing.
