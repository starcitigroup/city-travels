Business principle: Always use free or ultra-low-cost services wherever possible. 
This applies to hosting, databases, forms, email, and any other web or software infrastructure.

Guidelines:
1. Prioritize services with free tiers or generous quotas (e.g., Firebase, Vercel, Netlify, Formspree).
2. Avoid paid solutions unless absolutely necessary for core functionality.
3. Use open-source tools if they reduce costs and maintenance.
4. Prefer solutions that require minimal backend management.
5. Automation and advanced features are optional; manual workflows are acceptable if cheaper.
6. Every recommendation or technical solution should be filtered by cost-efficiency first.
7. Recommend scaling or upgrades only when necessary, and always note the cost impact.

Special notes for agents:
- Business Analyst: Propose solutions that are cost-effective.
- Solution Architect: Suggest tech stack choices that minimize expenses.
- Fullstack Dev: Use free-tier-friendly libraries and hosting.
- Marketing Content: Recommend marketing and communication tools that are free or very low cost.
- Product Manager: Prioritize MVP and low-cost experiments.
